What we do

Big company features, built with your business in mind.

Most off-the-shelf software asks you to change how you work. We start by learning how your business or nonprofit already works, and then we design the tools around it.

Custom app design

We design custom mobile apps, web-based databases, user portals, CRMs and other tools from the first sketch to a finished interface. Every screen is clear and easy to learn.

Professional websites

We build polished websites that are easy to update yourself, or we can manage them for you with a care plan. Your site will tell your story clearly and help visitors become customers.

AI onboarding

We find the tasks that AI can take off your plate, set up the tools, and show your team how to use them in plain language.

Process integration

Love Gmail? Want to keep using QuickBooks? Keep the parts of your process that already work. We build apps that connect to your favorite tools and support the way you want to work.

Online stores and payment processing

We build online stores with secure checkout that work with whichever payment processor you prefer. Your customers can browse, pay and track their orders, and your team sees every sale in one place.

Launch and ongoing support

After launch, we offer care plans to maintain your site. We watch how people use what we built and make improvements as your business grows.

Your check-in wingman.

Heading out on a hike, a first date or a late drive? ChickenWing lets you plan an outing with check-in times and choose a trusted contact, called your ChickenWing. If you miss a check-in, your contact is alerted automatically, even if your phone has died.

1

Easy check-ins. Checking in takes one tap on a large button, so it is easy to do while you are on the move.

2

Reliable alerts. Alerts are sent from our servers, so they still go out if your phone battery runs out.

Happy Home Supply

Everything a busy online shop needs in one place.

A CRM and seller interface for a home goods business that sells on several online marketplaces. Suppliers, inquiries, orders and inventory are all kept in one place. When a supplier fills out the inquiry form on the website, it is added to the CRM automatically.

1

Today’s tasks first. The dashboard opens with the work that needs attention today.

2

Automatic inquiries. Website inquiries go straight into supplier records, so nobody has to copy and paste them.

3

Organized the way the owner works. Suppliers and service vendors are kept in separate lists, and each one has its own history.

How we work

Our process is simple, and you are involved at every step.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We learn about your business, your team and the tasks that slow you down.

  2. 02

    Sketch

    We create clickable designs that you can try out and comment on before anything is built.

  3. 03

    Build

    We build the design using practical, affordable tools that can grow with you.

  4. 04

    Onboard

    We train your team on the new tools, including any AI tools that will save them time.

  5. 05

    Host and maintain

    We can hand off the finished project to your in-house tech team, or host and maintain it for you on a monthly care plan including minor updates and support requests through our ticketing system.

  6. 06

    Grow

    When you are ready for new features, we plan them with you and give you a clear quote before any work begins.
